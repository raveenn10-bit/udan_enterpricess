COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I charge a Zuboo electric bike at home? +

Every Zuboo bike comes with a dedicated smart charger that plugs directly into any standard domestic 230V / 13-Amp Sri Lankan wall socket. Most models feature easily removable battery packs so you can charge indoors inside your home, apartment, or office.

How much does it cost to fully charge a Zuboo scooter? +

A full battery charge typically consumes between 1.5 to 2.5 units (kWh) of electricity, which translates to approximately LKR 80 to LKR 180 under standard CEB domestic tariffs. For that small cost, you get up to 80 to 110 km of travel — delivering an incredible 80%+ savings compared to petrol.

Can I ride Zuboo electric bikes during the rainy monsoon season? +

Yes! Zuboo electric bikes are built with weather-sealed electronics and IP65/IP67 rated waterproof hub motors and controllers designed to handle tropical rain, damp conditions, and shallow wet puddles safely. However, like any two-wheeler, avoid submerging the battery compartment in deep floodwaters.

What warranty is provided by Udan Enterprises? +

We provide up to a 2-Year or 20,000 km warranty on high-capacity battery packs and electric motors, alongside a 3-Year chassis frame warranty on our flagship models (GT, The Tank, Paris, T11, T3, EX008, KB1). Full after-sales servicing is conducted by certified technicians at our Kurunduwatta, Galle facility.
