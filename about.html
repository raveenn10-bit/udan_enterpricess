THE UDAN ENTERPRISES MISSION

BORN TO TRANSFORM DAILY COMMUTING

Founded in Kurunduwatta, Galle, Udan Enterprises emerged from an urgent national imperative: to provide everyday Sri Lankan commuters, entrepreneurs, and families with a reliable, budget-friendly escape from soaring petrol prices and rising vehicle maintenance costs.

We partnered with Zuboo Electric Bikes — an internationally renowned pioneer in high-torque electric two-wheelers — to deliver smart scooters engineered specifically for the demands of Sri Lankan roads. From high-clearance suspensions to water-resistant motor hubs and high-capacity battery configurations, every Zuboo bike in our showroom offers unmatched durability and style.

Our flagship slogan, "JOIN US & CHARGE YOUR RIDE ⚡", encapsulates our pledge to lead Sri Lanka's southern province into a sustainable future.
