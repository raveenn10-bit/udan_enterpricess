## 1. Overview & Verification Protocol

This document serves as the master engineering and commercial specification register for all **8 Zuboo Electric Bike models** officially represented by **Udan Enterprises** in Sri Lanka.

> [!WARNING]
> **Provisional Data Notice:**
> The prices, battery chemistries, and technical dimensions documented herein were extracted and transcribed from promotional WhatsApp marketing flyers, importer brochures, and initial factory packing slips. Each model is flagged in code with `isPriceVerified: false`. Udan Enterprises staff must conduct physical vehicle audits at the Galle showroom prior to publishing formal financing sheets.

### 1.1 Specification Verification Checkpoints
Showroom staff must verify each model using the following five-point inspection:
1. **VIN & Spec Plate Audit:** Check the physical metal plate riveted to the head tube/chassis for exact motor rated wattage, voltage, and curb weight.
2. **Battery Pack Certification:** Confirm cell chemistry (Tianneng Lead-Acid AGM vs. Lithium Iron Phosphate / Li-ion), amp-hour (Ah) rating, and whether the battery is removable or fixed.
3. **Braking System Confirmation:** Verify whether the rear brake is hydraulic disc or cable drum.
4. **Charger Specifications:** Record charger input wattage, voltage, plug type (UK 3-pin standard for Sri Lanka), and full-charge duration.
5. **Retail Price Point (LKR):** Verify advertised price versus current customs duty, VAT, and showroom floor delivery fees.
